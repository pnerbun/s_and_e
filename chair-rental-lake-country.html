Questions

Chair Rental FAQ

What is chair rental at a salon?

Chair rental (also called booth rental) means you rent a station inside an established salon and run your own independent business from it — you set your own prices, keep your own earnings, choose your own products, and build your own client book. Smith + Elliot is a collective of independent stylists who rent chairs this way.

What's included when I rent a chair at Smith + Elliot?

A move-in-ready station in a beautifully restored, professional salon in downtown Delafield, in a supportive community of independent stylists. You bring your license, your products, and your clients; we provide the space and the setting.

Do I need my own clients and license to rent a chair?

Yes. Chair rental is for licensed cosmetologists running their own business, so you'll bring your license and your own book of clients. It's ideal for stylists ready to be their own boss rather than work on commission.
